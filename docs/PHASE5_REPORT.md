# Phase 5 Development Report — Purchases

## Completed

- `Vendor` model: master data for accounts payable — the mirror of `Customer` (Phase 4),
  plus an `is_vat_registered` flag (data a bookkeeper sets per vendor, not inferred).
- `PurchaseBill` / `PurchaseBillLine` models: Draft → Posted lifecycle, same immutability
  rule as sales invoices and journal entries.
- Purchase posting service (`app/services/purchases.py`) that composes the accounting
  engine (Phase 2) and tax engine (Phase 3), mirroring `app/services/sales.py` — with one
  addition specific to Philippine purchases: **expanded/creditable withholding tax**. When a
  bill line carries a `withholding_tax_rule_code`, the tax engine calculates the amount the
  business must withhold from the vendor and remit to the BIR instead of paying it out, so:
  - `grand_total` = subtotal + input VAT (what the vendor's invoice says is owed)
  - `amount_due_to_vendor` = `grand_total` − withholding tax (what actually gets paid)
  - the difference is credited to a Withholding Tax Payable liability, not paid in cash
- The resulting journal entry balances by construction — Debit Expense + Input VAT
  == Credit AP (net of withholding) + Credit Withholding Tax Payable — and is still
  re-validated by the accounting engine's own balance check, not just trusted.
- Three new configurable control accounts on `BusinessSettings`: `ap_account_id`,
  `input_vat_account_id`, `withholding_tax_payable_account_id` — each only required if a
  given bill actually needs it (e.g. a bill with no withholding never checks for a
  withholding account), so a business that doesn't do withholding isn't forced to configure
  an account it'll never use.
- API endpoints for vendors (create/list) and purchase bills (create draft/list/get/post).
- Frontend: Vendors view, Purchase Bills view (multi-line draft creation with separate VAT
  and withholding-tax rule pickers per line, list with Post action), Business Settings
  extended with the three new account pickers.

## Database Changes

Migration `53b80a61b462_phase5_purchases` adds `vendors`, `purchase_bills`,
`purchase_bill_lines`, and three nullable FK columns on `business_settings`.

**Same migration issue as Phase 4, caught and fixed proactively this time:** the
autogenerated migration again used plain `op.create_foreign_key(...)` to add FKs to the
already-existing `business_settings` table, which SQLite rejects with
`NotImplementedError: No support for ALTER of constraints`. Having hit this exact failure in
Phase 4, I edited the migration to use `op.batch_alter_table(...)` before ever running it —
verified with a full `rm -f dev.db && alembic upgrade head` from empty, running all five
migrations (Phases 1–5) in sequence without error, so this round there was no failed run to
report, just the fix applied up front.

## Tax/Accounting Rules Added

None new — Phase 5 reuses the same `TaxRule` mechanism from Phase 3 (both `VAT_STANDARD`
and, newly exercised here, a `Withholding`-type rule with an ATC code, e.g.
`WT_EWT_PROF_FEES`). No rates are hard-coded into the purchases module.

## Known Issues

- No bill void/reversal workflow yet, same gap as sales invoices in Phase 4 — would use the
  accounting engine's `reverse_entry()` plus a bill status change.
- No BIR Form 2307 (Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source) generation yet — that
  belongs to Phase 7 (BIR compliance), which will consume the `withholding_tax_amount`
  already being tracked per bill line.
- No AP aging or payment-application workflow — a bill is either unpaid (Posted, sitting in
  AP) or paid outside the system; payment application against AP is a later-phase concern,
  same as AR in Phase 4.
- No purchase order → bill matching (3-way match against a PO and receiving record) — this
  phase only covers the vendor bill itself.
- Like Phase 4's sales invoice lines, a purchase bill line supports at most one VAT rule and
  one withholding rule — no multi-component tax stacking beyond that.

## Next Phase

**Phase 6 — Banking**: bank accounts, cash receipts (customer payments applied against AR,
finally closing the loop opened in Phase 4), cash disbursements (vendor payments applied
against AP — including the withholding tax nuance from this phase, where the actual cash
paid out is `amount_due_to_vendor`, not the bill's `grand_total`), and bank reconciliation.
This is the phase where AR and AP balances actually get reduced to zero as invoices/bills
get paid, rather than sitting posted-but-unpaid as they do at the end of Phases 4 and 5.
